Course project note: this was a school-related group project completed in my final semester for the Infocomm System Project module.

In my final semester, I collaborated on this group project for the Infocomm System Project module. The platform was designed to strengthen student-staff collaboration for internships and PRISM industry-related projects by giving both groups a clearer way to share information and manage opportunities.

Students could upload resumes, list technical skills, and indicate their preferences, while staff had tools to manage profiles, opportunities, and edits. The platform also introduced a smart matchmaking algorithm to improve career exploration and placements by making it easier for students and staff to find strong fits.

My contributions focused on both design and implementation. I worked on the UI/UX of the application and coded both the front-end and back-end for the navigation and routing functions across the platform.

Platform goals

  • Improve collaboration between students and staff during internship and PRISM project planning.
  • Give students a structured way to present resumes, technical skills, and personal preferences.
  • Make it easier for staff to review information and manage opportunities in one shared platform.

Core workflow

  • Students could maintain profiles that captured resumes, skills, and stated interests.
  • Staff could manage profiles, opportunity listings, and edit-related actions.
  • A smart matchmaking flow helped connect students to more relevant placements and project options.

My contribution

  • Designed the UI/UX to support a clearer user journey for both student and staff users.
  • Built front-end navigation so users could move smoothly between the main application views.
  • Worked on back-end routing functions to support the platform's page flow and connected experience.
